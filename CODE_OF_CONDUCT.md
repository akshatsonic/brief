# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation harassment-free for everyone. Be respectful, constructive, and
considerate. Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, publishing private information,
and other unprofessional behavior are unacceptable.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and restrict participants whose behavior is
harmful. Report incidents privately through the repository's security contact mechanism. This
policy follows the intent of Contributor Covenant 2.1.
